About Me

Operations research for transportation, logistics, and energy systems

I am a Ph.D. researcher in Industrial Engineering at Wichita State University. My work focuses on optimization models and solution algorithms for transportation, logistics, vehicle-routing, and energy-infrastructure problems.

My research combines mathematical programming, metaheuristics, decomposition methods, simulation, and data-driven decision making.
