About

Optimization for real-world transportation and logistics systems

I am a Ph.D. researcher in Industrial Engineering at Wichita State University. My research focuses on combinatorial optimization problems in transportation, logistics, and energy systems. I am particularly interested in developing mathematical models and efficient solution algorithms for complex routing and infrastructure-planning problems.

Alongside research, I teach undergraduate engineering courses and enjoy translating technical optimization concepts into practical, understandable decision-making tools.
