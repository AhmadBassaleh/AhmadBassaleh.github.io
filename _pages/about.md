## About Me

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Industrial, Systems, and Manufacturing Engineering at [Wichita State University](https://www.wichita.edu/), working under the advisement of [Prof. Mehmet Bayram Yildirim](https://www.wichita.edu/). My research centers on combinatorial optimization in transportation, spanning rich vehicle routing problem (VRP) variants, exact decomposition methods such as Benders decomposition and column generation, and agent-based modeling of gig-economy and electric vehicle charging systems. I completed my B.S. and M.S. in Industrial Engineering at [Ozyegin University](https://www.ozyegin.edu.tr/en) in Istanbul, Turkey, before beginning my Ph.D. at WSU in 2024.

Alongside my research, I serve as a graduate teaching assistant for Operations Research II and Engineering Economy, and I was awarded the Graduate Teaching Assistant Award for Excellence in Teaching in 2025. I also work as an Energy Auditor through WSU's Industrial Training and Assessment Center (ITAC), leading energy efficiency assessments for manufacturing facilities and supporting USDA REAP grant education for industry partners. I am involved in campus leadership through the WSU Student Government Association's engineering senate, and I am a member of the WSU SME Student Chapter.

### Research Interests

* Combinatorial optimization and vehicle routing problems
* Exact decomposition methods (Benders decomposition, column generation, branch-and-price)
* Electric vehicle charging infrastructure planning
* Agent-based modeling and simulation
* Metaheuristics and reinforcement learning for routing problems
* Quantum optimization
