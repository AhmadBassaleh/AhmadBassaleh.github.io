Ahmad Bassaleh

Ph.D. Candidate, Industrial, Systems, and Manufacturing Engineering · Wichita State University

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Industrial, Systems, and Manufacturing Engineering at Wichita State University, working under the advisement of Prof. Mehmet Bayram Yildirim. My research centers on combinatorial optimization in transportation, spanning rich vehicle routing problem (VRP) variants, exact decomposition methods such as Benders decomposition and column generation, and agent-based modeling of gig-economy and electric vehicle charging systems. I completed my B.S. and M.S. in Industrial Engineering at Ozyegin University in Istanbul, Turkey, before beginning my Ph.D. at WSU in 2024.

Alongside my research, I serve as a graduate teaching assistant for Operations Research II and Engineering Economy, and I was awarded the Graduate Teaching Assistant Award for Excellence in Teaching in 2025. I also work as an Energy Auditor through WSU's Industrial Training and Assessment Center (ITAC), leading energy efficiency assessments for manufacturing facilities and supporting USDA REAP grant education for industry partners. I am involved in campus leadership through the WSU Student Government Association's engineering senate, and I am a member of the WSU SME Student Chapter.
