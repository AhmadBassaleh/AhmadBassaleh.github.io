My research develops exact and heuristic optimization methods for hard combinatorial problems in transportation and logistics, with a particular focus on electric vehicle systems, gig-economy platforms, and large-scale routing.

### Research Interests

* **Rich Vehicle Routing Problems** — time-dependent, electric, and trailer-based VRP variants; time-window and tariff-constrained routing.
* **Exact Decomposition Methods** — Dantzig-Wolfe decomposition and column generation with label-setting dynamic programming pricing subproblems; Benders decomposition (integer L-shaped method) for large-scale MILPs.
* **Electric Vehicle Charging Infrastructure** — trajectory-based charging infrastructure planning and optimization under energy and timing constraints.
* **Agent-Based Modeling** — simulation of gig-economy transportation systems and driver behavior calibrated to real-world data.
* **Metaheuristics and Learning-Based Methods** — simulated annealing, reinforcement learning (Q-learning, DQN), and hybrid frameworks for routing problems.
* **Quantum Optimization** — quantum approaches to coordinated routing problems.
